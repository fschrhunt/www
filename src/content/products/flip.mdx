Notes on the back of your windows.

Sometimes a note belongs with the thing you're doing. A bug to revisit in your editor. A question to keep beside your browser. Flip gives that note a place on the back of the app's window.

Option-click an empty part of the title bar and the window flips over to a plain-text note. Write something, then press Escape to return. There's one note per app, shared across its windows, so opening another window brings you back to the same text.

You can also open Flip from the menu bar to see all your notes together. Search them, edit them, or mark one done. The library and the back of the window edit the same note, and changes save automatically on your Mac.

Flip is still in development for macOS 14 and newer. The repository has build instructions and a guide to the permissions it needs. Screen Recording is optional; without it, the note opens instantly instead of turning over.
