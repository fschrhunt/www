# Find the thing that still feels wrong

Read [the site brief](../../site.md). Inspect the changed page in context,
including its entry point. Stay within the requested review scope. A critique
request produces findings; a request to fix or polish authorizes scoped edits.

An intentional departure from the homepage is not a defect. Assess the page
against its own idea and the user's direction. Do not recommend monochrome,
smaller type, or the letter layout simply because those already exist.

Look for evidence, not a numerical taste score:

- Does the page say something absent from the homepage? Can the reader explain
  the product after reading its description?
- Is a funny line rooted in something true, or could it be pasted onto any
  developer's website? Does the joke get in the way of an action?
- Does the concept survive beyond the opening screen? Is the direction specific
  to this content, or a reference copied without a reason? Did caution flatten
  the most interesting part? Identify where it could be developed, not just cut.
- Do reading edges, text sizes, and gaps establish a clear order? Is the mobile
  composition considered? Is an illustration clearly an illustration?
- During actual hover frames, can scrambled glyphs reach the arrow? Does any
  text or date shift? Check keyboard focus and pointer exit as well as hover.
- Does entrance motion replay on navigation without replaying on local form
  updates? With reduced motion, does the page remain visible and usable?
- Can someone reach the last link without the floating hub blocking it? Do
  contact, project destinations, and status claims match what actually happens?

Select checks relevant to the change. Do not run the whole list for a comma.
Distinguish a reproducible defect from a design preference. Cite the location
and observed state, explain the consequence, and propose a concrete correction.
Skip compliments and speculative busywork. If nothing material is wrong, say so.

When authorized to fix, make the smallest changes that address the findings,
then revisit the affected state. Report remaining uncertainties honestly.
Local checks are not evidence that the deployed site or native Safari works.
